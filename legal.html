Privacy & terms

Last updated: November 2026

Privacy

duha is a demo crypto dashboard. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers. Two things are stored so the product works:

  • Your watchlist. A random, HttpOnly session cookie identifies this browser. The coins you star are saved in a private SQLite database under that random ID. The cookie lasts up to 12 months. Clear cookies to reset it.
  • Newsletter signups. If you submit the demo newsletter form, the email address is stored in the same database. The form is labelled as a demo — no emails are sent unless the operator connects an email provider.

Third parties

Market data is fetched from CoinGecko's public API by the duha server, and typography is loaded from Google Fonts. Those providers may see the server's or your browser's IP address according to their own policies. No personal data is sold or shared.

Your choices

You can clear the watchlist by clearing cookies, and you can ask for a newsletter address to be removed by contacting us. Server logs contain no email addresses.

Terms

duha is an independent demo project and is not affiliated with CoinGecko. Market data is indicative, may be delayed, and can be wrong. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, or legal advice.

The service is provided as-is, without warranties. Do not rely on it for trading decisions. Don't abuse the API, attempt to break the service, or scrape it at volume. These terms may change as the project evolves.
