Crypto basics

The ideas behind the charts.

Four short answers to the questions that explain most of what you see on this page — no jargon, no hype.

Explore more at CoinGecko Learn
What actually is a cryptocurrency?

A digital asset recorded on a blockchain — a shared ledger maintained by many computers instead of one central issuer. It can move value without a bank in the middle, and its supply is set by code.

Why do crypto prices move so much?

Crypto trades 24/7 in markets that are smaller and less liquid than major stock markets. News, regulation, leverage, and large trades can all move prices quickly in both directions.

What does market cap mean?

Market cap is price multiplied by circulating supply. It's a rough size gauge, not the amount of money invested in a coin — which is why a low-priced asset can still be worth more than a high-priced one.

Are stablecoins actually stable?

Most aim to hold a 1:1 peg to a fiat currency using reserves or algorithms. They usually trade near $1, but they can — and have — lost their peg under stress.
